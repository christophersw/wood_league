How to read this chart

LC0’s neural-network probability distribution per ply. The three bands always sum to 100%: one side’s win chance on the bottom, draw chance in the middle, the other side’s win chance on top. Flipping the board swaps which side is on which side.

The strip beneath the chart marks each ply’s move quality (best → blunder) using the same palette as the cards above.

Click any band or strip cell to jump to that ply.
