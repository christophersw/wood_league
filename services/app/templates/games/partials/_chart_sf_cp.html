How to read this chart

Each bar stacks the previous ply’s score (dark green) with this move’s change. The change segment carries the move-quality colour so blunders, mistakes, and brilliancies pop.

The y-axis runs 0–12 pawns in both directions. The top and bottom labels say which side’s advantage lives in each half; flipping the board flips which side sits on top.

Click any bar to jump to that ply.
